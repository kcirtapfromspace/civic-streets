# Direct cash: founder and small-business funding

Checked September 14, 2026. This extends the earlier geography-specific research. No accounts, applications, memberships, or messages were submitted. Eligibility depends on facts not yet known: applicant age/education, legal business status, financial circumstances, and whether a genuinely volunteer/nonprofit project exists. A willingness to pilot somewhere does not change founder-residency or business-eligibility rules.

## Cash-first recommendation

Keep Emergent Ventures in the main queue. Add Verizon Digital Ready if Curbwise is already an eligible U.S. for-profit business, and Solo Grants if a small specific build expense is the barrier. Medici, Bagel, and Thiel require founder eligibility checks. Pollination supports a separate genuinely volunteer public-benefit activity. NASE is more attractive for an existing member than someone buying membership solely for a grant chance.

## Seven new leads

### 1. Verizon / LISC Small Business Digital Ready — $10,000 cash grant

**Open:** Current 2026 grant page accepts applications; monthly selections June–December; final decisions by January 12, 2027. Owners 18+ of for-profit businesses in the U.S., Puerto Rico, or U.S. Virgin Islands; nonprofits excluded. Complete two free eligible courses/events to unlock the application. The detailed FAQ gives January 1–December 7, 2026 as the resource-completion window. [Current rules and registration/application route](https://digitalready.verizonwireless.com/funding/details).

**Timing/fit:** No promised payout date. Selection begins with an anonymous drawing, then geography/industry balancing and review; this is a useful extra application, not predictable cash. Our proposed use: approved digital tools, product reliability, or customer acquisition for a real business. Exact budget needs approval.

**Source conflict resolved:** A generic support FAQ still says no open grant. The specific 2026 page and [LISC grant administrator](https://www.lisc.org/our-initiatives/small-business/our-work/verizon-small-business-digital-ready/grant-program/) both explicitly say open. Use the specific campaign page, not the generic FAQ. LISC page was verified by direct download.

### 2. Solo Grants — $100–$1,000 cash; particularly actionable

**Open:** Official site invites applications without a fixed deadline. One builder, any age, worldwide; financial need matters. Grants pay specific parts, compute, materials, and other expenses that unblock a first working version. No equity, repayment, or future investment rights. Advertised average is about ten days from application to funding; this is an average, not a guarantee. [Official program](https://www.sologrants.com/).

**Our proposed ask:** A modest, itemized build budget with actual vendor prices, current demo, and the precise next milestone. Hosting/compute for a defined test could fit. The program's own [guide](https://sologrants.com/guide/solo-grant) excludes salary, runway, and market research. Curbwise already has a prototype: explain the specific unfinished capability and financial barrier truthfully.

**Apply:** [Official linked Airtable form](https://airtable.com/appHaOk5VRk50CpcF/pag93ybs2R8OfxZXD/form). Link reached; form fields were not inspected. Do not confuse this grant with the separate $100,000 Solo Founders equity accelerator.

### 3. 1517 Medici Project — minimum $1,000 cash stipend

**Open invitation:** No fixed deadline published. Supports high-school/college students and dropouts with projects outside school assignments. Teams must be based in North America for direct grants. Do not assume this founder qualifies.

**Terms/timing:** No equity or IP claim; money described as a Venmo payment. No review/payout turnaround promised. Tech and software projects are explicitly among past areas supported.

**Application:** The [official Medici page](https://www.1517fund.com/medici) requests a five-minute Loom video sent to a team member at the published firstname@1517fund.com address pattern. Cover personal background, why the project matters, demonstrated work, and what the grant unlocks. Verify the actual named recipient before sending. This is a grant; later venture investment is separate and optional. No outreach performed.

### 4. Bagel Fund — $100–$500 cash microgrant

**Open invitation:** Site currently invites applications for young builders; no fixed cutoff or hard age limit is published on the page. Geography limits and payment mechanics are not stated; do not assume universal eligibility. The site lists a real portfolio including software and AI projects and identifies the organization as a 501(c)(3).

**Timing:** It promises an email within 48 hours if the project is a fit. That is a positive-response target, not a promise of a decision or payment for every applicant.

**Our fit assessment:** Small prototype expenses might fit if the actual founder meets its youth focus. [Official program](https://bagel.fund/), [official linked application](https://airtable.com/appbBigvOyUCojEAU/pagngFqHu5eeoAxhg/form). Linked form reached; fields not inspected. Keep this behind Solo unless age/background fit is clear.

### 5. The Pollination Project — up to $500; volunteer public-benefit work

**Open/eligible:** Rolling, worldwide individuals/informal groups/nonprofits; organization budget below $50,000, project below $10,000, no paid staff, no personal-profit design. Some professional contractors are allowed, but grant money cannot pay the grantee or their family for services. Prior recipients/groups cannot receive another seed grant.

**Timing:** Complete applications received before month-end are normally reviewed the following month, with decisions at that month's end. September completion therefore suggests late October/early November decision, not immediate cash. Payout date unspecified.

**Our fit assessment:** A small volunteer-led public street-design activity could fit; ordinary founder payroll or commercial platform runway cannot. The fund also prohibits funding events serving animal products, among other values-based restrictions. Review the full rules before designing an event. [Official rules and application](https://thepollinationproject.org/apply/).

### 6. Thiel Fellowship — $250,000 over two years; narrow founder gate

**Open:** Applications accepted year-round. Applicant must be 22 or younger and have no university degree. Students may apply but must leave school to accept. Accepts individuals worldwide; relocation to SF is not required. No company incorporation required, but meaningful progress on a concrete vision is.

**Cash/terms:** Current official amount is $250,000 spread over two years, no equity. Older $100,000/$200,000 summaries are stale. No selection or first-payment turnaround promised.

**Action:** Only pursue if the founder already meets the requirements and wants the fellowship's commitment. Do not alter education plans merely for this funding list. [Official FAQ](https://thielfellowship.org/faq), [official application portal](https://thielfellowship.my.site.com/apply/s/). Portal linked correctly but returned a CSS/loading error to research tooling; eligibility comes from the official FAQ.

### 7. NASE Growth Grants — up to $4,000; paid membership gate

**Open:** Accepts applications year-round. NASE membership in good standing is required. Annual members may apply immediately; monthly members must wait 90 days. Do not buy membership on the assumption a grant will follow.

**Timing:** July–September applications reviewed in October; October–December reviewed in January. Grants are funded within 30 days after signed usage and required regulatory documents arrive. That is after selection and paperwork, not 30 days from application.

**Use/fit:** Website creation, software/equipment, marketing, and hiring are explicitly eligible; debt and rent/mortgage payments excluded. New businesses may submit projected financials. This could fit an actual Curbwise business. Needs business plan, grant-use statement, financials/projections, and photo. [Official rules and member application route](https://www.nase.org/become-a-member/member-benefits/business-resources/growth-grants). Membership fee not priced in this research; existing membership would improve the economics.
