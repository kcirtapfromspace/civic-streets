# Curbwise — national funding sprint

**Updated September 14, 2026, following founder direction: pilot anywhere that offers a viable funding path.** This supersedes the previous Denver-first ordering. The funding search is now nationwide, with geography-flexible founder grants first and local partner development in parallel.

## Objective and working rules

**30-day targets:** put 6–10 qualified requests in motion, pursue the first $1,000–$5,000 in usable project cash, and have at least two larger requests under review. These are operating targets, not expected awards. No applications have been submitted and no money is committed.

Prioritize eligibility, payment timing, useful work covered, and preparation effort. Small grants are worthwhile when they pay for a defined milestone with limited application work. Larger research awards remain a separate pipeline because they may require technical novelty, match money, relocation, or a much later start.

Pilot location is flexible. Applicant residence, entity type, age, and a host's authority must still be accurate. Choose a community with real participants and affordable delivery as part of pursuing the grant. The founder's legal structure remains unanswered; the queue preserves both individual and business routes.

## C. Quick screens and longer prospects

- **Founder-background screen:** Medici (minimum $1,000), Bagel ($100–$500), and Thiel ($250,000 over two years) have student/youth/education gates. Check actual facts once, then apply or remove them. Do not infer founder age or education. [Rules and application routes](research-direct-cash.md).
- **Matching campaigns:** Sustainable CT can match up to $6,000, but requires new donations and eligible local delivery. It is not an upfront $6,000 check. Use only with a host that can recruit donors. [Details](research-community-cash.md).
- **NSF:** A Project Pitch is worth a brief technical-fit assessment only if there is a genuine high-risk research innovation. Ordinary app development does not qualify. Maine and Massachusetts programs require substantial business-location/match commitments, and larger pilot awards can start in 2027. [Verified funded-pilot routes](research-funded-pilots.md).
- **DRK:** Up to $300,000 over three years, through grants or investment; rolling but lengthy review. Current criteria require post-pilot impact evidence and exclude LLCs and pre-pilot organizations. Keep it after evidence and entity fit, not in the first-cash target. [Criteria](https://www.drkfoundation.org/apply/faq/), [instrument and review schedule](https://www.drkfoundation.org/apply/overview/).
- **Future cycles:** AARP, America Walks, LA2050, and relevant open-source calls remain a watchlist. Their closed/currently unverified cycles do not count toward live requests. [Existing research](research-national.md).

## Prepared narrative: business-funding version

**For Verizon/NASE if the applicant is an eligible business; this is source material, not verified private form wording.**

Curbwise is an early-stage street-design and community-participation product. Its prototype helps people explore street cross-sections, compare concepts, and generate discussion reports. We are preparing the first funded pilot with a local organization, with location determined by partner demand and delivery readiness. Our immediate priorities are reliable reporting and storage, accessible workflows, and a repeatable way to turn residents' observations into a useful brief.

A $10,000 grant would support a proposed $4,000 in product reliability work, $2,000 in accessibility improvements, $2,000 in pilot onboarding and facilitation, $1,000 in hosting/tools, and $1,000 in documentation and customer acquisition. These categories require funder approval and real quotations. The grant would help test whether organizations will pay for facilitation and reporting while community participants can contribute without a fee. Current revenue, users, company history, owner background, and partnerships must be supplied accurately; no traction is asserted here.

**For a $4,000 NASE request:** choose the highest-priority eligible subset, such as quoted website/software reliability work, and document the specific business need and measurable benefit. Do not submit the $10,000 budget unchanged.

## Parallel cash route: a paid hosted pilot

Offer a **proposed $2,500 remote-assisted pilot** to organizations with an actual budget. This is a price to validate, not a published market rate or signed contract. Scope: one host planning call, one resident workshop, three discussion concepts, one accessible brief, and one report-back. Host recruits residents and supplies local logistics; travel and construction are outside this quoted scope.

Proposed commercial terms: $1,250 at kickoff and $1,250 at delivery, subject to the buyer's actual purchasing rules. If a public agency cannot advance money, use its approved invoice schedule and account for the cash gap. A grant-funded host must confirm that Curbwise's service and the vendor arrangement are allowable. No unrestricted grant is implied.

**Host inquiry draft:**

> We are looking for a local partner to run a small Curbwise street-design pilot. Your residents choose the concern; we provide supported concept exploration and an illustrated brief. We can deliver remotely with a local co-lead and are flexible about location. A proposed paid pilot is $2,500, and we have also identified grants that could support eligible community activities. Would your organization have a relevant program budget, or interest in leading a grant application? The first discussion would establish the neighborhood, responsible staff member, participant recruitment, and intended recipient of the brief.

No outreach has been sent. Grant awards, donor matches, reimbursable budgets, investment, credits, and signed service revenue must remain separately tracked. Count only cleared cash as cash available; distinguish funds committed to public activities from money available for product operations.
